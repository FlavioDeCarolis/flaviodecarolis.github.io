Cross-Faculty Collaboration
—
"Tussentijdse evaluatie van de wettelijke bedenktijd voor het bestuur van beursvennootschappen"
Interim evaluation, commissioned by the WODC (Dutch Ministry of Justice and Security), of the statutory reflection period ("bedenktijd") that lets the board of a listed company delay its response to a hostile takeover bid or activist shareholder proposal by up to 250 days. Contributed the event-study design testing whether the reform's introduction (May 2021) moved share prices of Dutch listed companies — it found no significant effect.
