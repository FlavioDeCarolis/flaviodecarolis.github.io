Flavio De Carolis

Postdoctoral Researcher, International Finance

Geneva Finance Research Institute · University of Geneva

Research on international asset pricing, with applications to climate and geopolitical risk.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Geneva Finance Research Institute, University of Geneva. My work sits in international asset pricing, studying how climate and geopolitical risk get priced into firms' capital structures and asset values. My PhD thesis at Maastricht University — "Let's get physical: How floods and storms affect asset prices and capital structures" — traced this question through firm-level data across the euro area. I'm also a Visiting Researcher in the Sustainability Team at the Statistics Department of De Nederlandsche Bank, and a permanent Nuvolos PhD Fellow. I'm an enthusiastic adopter of AI, and I spend part of my time working on how to put it to use in research and industry practice.
