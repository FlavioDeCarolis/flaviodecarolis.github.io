Research Interests

My work is broadly in international finance and asset pricing, with a recurring focus on how physical and geopolitical shocks get priced into firms' capital structures and valuations — plus a standing interest in the corporate-finance and credit-risk questions that sit next door.

01

International Finance & Asset Pricing

How cross-border capital flows, exchange-rate exposure and country-level risk factors are reflected in the pricing of firms and assets across markets.

02

Climate & Geopolitical Risk

Physical climate risk — floods and storms, specifically — and its transmission into asset prices and capital structure, alongside geopolitical risk as a related pricing factor. My PhD work built firm-level exposure measures for euro-area firms by linking pollutant-release registries (EPRTR) with business registers (RIAD) through fuzzy matching, and a related paper with Vinzenz Peters studies how European SMEs' corporate finance and economic resilience respond to flood exposure.

03

Corporate Finance

Capital structure decisions under physical and financial stress, particularly for small and medium-sized enterprises, which are more exposed and less studied than large listed firms.

04

Credit Scoring

How risk factors — including climate exposure — feed into firm-level credit assessment, and what that means for lenders and financial stability more broadly.
